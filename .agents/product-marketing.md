## Product and positioning

원마케팅 connects brands with campaign participants and supports recruitment, content production, and publication checks in one workflow. The public service describes both direct recruitment and managed production/operation. Category: campaign operations and content marketing service. Do not describe it as a guaranteed sales or ranking engine.

## Audiences and jobs

- Advertisers: choose a service, supply their product facts/assets, coordinate campaign and content work, review deliverables, and track payment/settlement status.
- Participants: find campaigns, apply, perform the agreed work, submit publication evidence, and check settlement status.
- Administrators: manage campaign eligibility, production briefs/approvals, review evidence, handle support and settlement operations.

Specific industries, company size, strongest acquisition channel, purchase objections, and customer verbatim language are not validated. Do not invent personas, interviews, quotations, or market share.

## Message and voice

Core message from the public pages: “모집부터 콘텐츠 게시 확인까지, 원마케팅에서.” Use clear, concise Korean with concrete actions and visible next steps. Distinguish requesting, paying, approving, generating, publishing, and settling: completing one does not prove the others occurred. Explain limitations in the context where they matter.

## Visual/content rules

For client blog work, use only that client's approved product facts and source images. Preserve actual product shape, labels, quantity, and materials. Reference brand names, prices, slogans, and unverified claims must not migrate into client output. Default to text-free article photography. Optional promotional posters use approved editable copy rendered separately; compare the result against source assets and inspect mobile readability before delivery. See `docs/blog-image-guidance-2026-11-08.md` for implementation and boundaries.

## Funnel and measurement

Separate service discovery, registration, campaign application/order, payment, content approval, publication verification, and settlement. Measure each against its own recorded event; no baseline conversion, retention, CAC, or revenue target has been established in this document. Marketing experiments need a concrete hypothesis and measured result, not invented uplift.
